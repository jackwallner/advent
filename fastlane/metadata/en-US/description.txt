Advent Doors turns December into 24 small surprises.

Pick what you love: self care, family fun, fitness, music, puzzles, food, humor and books. Pick a budget: free, low, medium or high. Advent Doors fills 24 doors with things to do, read or think about, and opens one each morning from December 1 to Christmas Eve. No peeking ahead.

Open a door, try it, and give it a few stars. Riddles hide their answers and jokes hide their punchlines until you tap.

Make one for someone
Build a calendar for a grandchild, a friend far away or your whole family, add a note, and send it as a link. They open it in any browser and get a new door every day. No app or account needed on their end.

Every door is written ahead of time and the whole calendar lives on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Morning reminders let you know when a new door is ready.
